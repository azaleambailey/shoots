Dara

I met Dara while studying abroad in Florence, Italy. I was attending a mixer on our apartment's rooftop. I was chatting with an American girl about my hometown friends and I was curious about Kazakhstan because of some enticing Instagram reels. She mentioned meeting a girl at this party from Kazakhstan and I asked her to point her out. I walked towards the girl and said "So I heard you're from Kazakhstan." She responded warmly to my odd statement and we ended up gabbing the night away.

During our conversation, Dara mentioned previously working as a model, but she needed new photos for her portfolio. I had only finished my second week at the art school I was attending, but I offered my services anyway. That weekend, we did a four hour photoshoot at my program's studio.

This was novel for me as I never acted as a professional photographer before, but that was not the only thing I gained from this experience. During my time in Italy, Dara and I became inseparable. From photoshoots, to late night kebabs, to dancing, we spent almost every day together. Dara was the catalyst to my becoming a portrait photographer, but I am forever more grateful for our beautiful friendship.

This was our second photoshoot together. Beforehand, Dara excitedly showed me a Pinterest board full of ideas. She was aesthetically inspired by the location of our initial photoshoot (the trash area of my art school) and curated a sexy industrial grunge Pinterest board. I was enthused to see that she brought three different outfits and an array of accessories. The vision of this photoshoot was a collaborative effort. Between each set of photos, I showed Dara the shots on my camera and we brainstormed what to do next. I found myself particularly thrilled during this shoot because unlike modeling digitals, there were no rules to follow. For me, this photoshoot was about mastering my portraiture skills and having fun with a friend. My favorite memory from this experience was Dara and I rejoicing about how good the photos looked over post photoshoot gelato.
